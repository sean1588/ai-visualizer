Mise.Quickstart

From rows to a plate in one pass.

  1. Open the app. No account.
  2. Drop a CSV/JSON file, paste rows, or fetch a public GET URL.
  3. Optional notes nudge the layout. They are guidance, not commands.
  4. Render. Schema is inferred locally. A small sample is sent once for layout.
  5. Ask The Chef to edit. Transforms (sort, limit) show as chips — they are not magic.
  6. Export a PNG, the recipe JSON, or table CSV / Markdown. Saves stay in this browser.

Recipes can be dropped back onto the plate and run against new data without asking the model again.
