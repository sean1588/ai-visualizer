Two doorways · one product

Bring the data in. Keep the recipe.

Mise is a dashboard renderer, not a warehouse. It samples your rows to design a layout, stores finished dashboards in this browser, and can refresh an HTTP-backed dashboard without asking the model to plan it again.

For analysts
Doorway 01 · Snapshot

Drop a file. See a dashboard.

CSV, JSON, or paste. Mise infers the schema, asks for a layout recipe, renders KPIs and charts, and keeps the finished dashboard in this browser so you can come back to it later.

Made for the way you work today

Two roles, one renderer.

Analyst

"I have a CSV due at 3pm."

Drop it. Get a dashboard. Export a PNG for the deck or download the recipe as JSON. If the layout needs polish, ask The Chef to hide a note, promote a KPI, or swap a chart.

"It went from rows to something I could talk through."
Pipeline owner

"I have a read API. I want a chart on top of it."

Paste the endpoint. Mise fetches, parses, renders, and remembers the source URL with the saved dashboard. Hit refresh when the upstream data changes.

"A dashboard pointed at our API, without building dashboard infra."
A note on what we keep

The server plans. Your browser keeps.

When you render a dashboard, Mise sends sampled data to a small inference service to decide which widgets to render and where. For public URLs, a lightweight proxy fetches the endpoint so CORS does not get in your way. The saved dashboard, source URL, rows, and recipe live in your browser storage, not in a hosted account.

No accounts · no hosted dashboard database · browser-local saves · PNG and recipe export
